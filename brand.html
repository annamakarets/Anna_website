makflowers

makflowers is a creative flower brand focused on soft, elegant, and modern floral design. The idea of the brand comes from my passion for flowers and my interest in branding and aesthetics.

makflowers offers bouquets, gift boxes, and flowers for special events. Each composition is designed to feel personal, stylish, and unique.

The brand follows a soft feminine and minimalist style, using natural colors like pink, beige, and green to create a calm and romantic feeling.
